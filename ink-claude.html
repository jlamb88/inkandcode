// the cost of bad design

What’s the Cost of
Bad Design?

As a business owner it’s easier to understand how something helps than what might hurt. Here’s what poor design actually costs you:

✕

Damages Credibility

First impressions matter. Poor visuals make your business look unprofessional and unreliable before you say a word.

✕

Hurts Brand Recognition

Inconsistent or forgettable design means your business doesn’t stick in anyone’s mind long enough to matter.

✕

Reduces Engagement

Weak, generic design gets ignored. Your audience scrolls past without a second thought — and goes to your competitor.

✕

Misses the Right Clients

The wrong look attracts the wrong audience — and drives away the ones you actually want to work with.
